## Human hinge

The three doors are not ranked recommendations.

- **TAKE** — perform the bounded representation-change experiment.
- **HOLD** — preserve the unresolved problem without acting yet.
- **PASS** — decline the door without pretending non-participation is failure.

DVOTE records the selected disposition plus the reader's own witness.

It does not infer what the witness means.

## Crossing boundary

The exported object is `dvote.witness-receipt.v1`.

Export makes the receipt portable. It does not give the receiver permission to reinterpret, execute, publish, or erase it.

The paired STATIC OS experiment consumes this exact carrier as source material and keeps source, interpretation, reorientation, and later edition material distinct.
