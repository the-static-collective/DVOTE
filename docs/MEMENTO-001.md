# MEMENTO 001

MEMENTO binds DVOTE receipts into a portable book.

It does not invent narrative, assign spiritual meaning, or summarize a person's life from above. It composes only from what the reader actually witnessed.

## Core rule

**Receipt = what happened, not what DVOTE claims it meant.**

A MEMENTO may reorganize receipts for reading, but it may not convert recurrence into authority.

## Editions

Milestone editions appear at **7**, **21**, and **42** receipts.

These milestones are invitations, not gates. The reader can choose **NOW** after the first receipt and generate a current edition from everything witnessed so far. No streak is required, and missing days do not invalidate the book.

## Edition contents

A MEMENTO contains:

- a cover
- one page per receipt
- inventory of deliberately carried objects
- repeated words detected across witness notes and carried objects
- a colophon preserving the non-interpretation law

Receipt pages preserve campaign day, encounter, door crossed, note, carried object when present, weather, and calendar date.

## Portable forms

- **Print / Save PDF** opens a print-ready standalone edition.
- **HTML** creates a standalone readable and printable book with no runtime dependency.
- **JSON** creates a `dvote.memento.v1` bundle containing campaign provenance, edition metadata, original receipts, inventory, and lexical recurrence.

## Prohibitions

MEMENTO does not:

- score faith or virtue
- infer divine messages from recurrence
- punish missed days
- rewrite witness notes into a cleaner story
- claim repeated words or objects are significant
- require a milestone before the reader may export their own material

## Architecture

**Campaign owns the book. Runtime owns the life. MEMENTO binds the traces.**

The campaign proposes.
The reader crosses.
The receipt witnesses.
MEMENTO preserves.
